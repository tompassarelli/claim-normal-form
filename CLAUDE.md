## Documentation rhythm

As we work through developments, maintain two living records:

- **`docs/devlog/`** — Key inflection points: when understanding shifts,
  when numbers surprise us, when direction changes. Write down reasoning
  and reflections, not just what happened. Each entry is a snapshot of
  understanding at the time. Update `docs/devlog/README.md` index.

- **`docs/experiments/`** — Empirical results with honest analysis.
  Save raw numbers, explain what they mean, note what would change
  the story. Results go in subdirectories (e.g. `e1-scripted/`).

- **`docs/todo.md`** — Living roadmap. Update status (DONE/NOW/NEXT/LATER)
  as work progresses.
